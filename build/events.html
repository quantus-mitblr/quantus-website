Quantum Leap 2.0

Quantum Leap 2.0 is an onboarding workshop for first-years to demystify quantum computing. We cover qubits, superposition, and basic linear algebra, then run hands-on labs in Qiskit to simulate simple algorithms (Deutsch–Jozsa, Grover) and circuits. No prior experience required—bring a laptop; we'll guide setup and share starter notebooks.
